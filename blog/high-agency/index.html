culture · business · 9 min

Some people happen to life. Everyone else waits for life to happen to them. The difference has a name, and you can train it.

There is a test for this. You wake up in a jail cell in a foreign country. You get one phone call. Who do you pick?

The idea comes from George Mack's essay High Agency. What follows is my own take, with toys.

The tricycle

High agency is not one trait. It is three skills that rarely show up in the same person: clear thinking, bias to action, and disagreeability. Remove any one of them and the whole thing tips over.
