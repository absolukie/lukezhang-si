How AI Keeps Getting Smarter

llmsaiResearched October 2026

Models are not just getting bigger anymore. Six engines keep pushing AI forward, and the biggest one in 2026 is post-training: teaching models to reason instead of just making them larger. This primer covers the six engines, the landmark moments since 2024, what eight leading voices expect by 2029, and where they genuinely disagree.

1. The brief

For years the story was simple: more compute, more data, more parameters, better model. That story still rhymes, but the action moved. In 2025 and 2026 the center of gravity shifted from pre-training to post-training and inference: GPT-5 used less training compute than GPT-4.5, while DeepSeek-R1 jumped AIME scores from 15.6% to 71.0% with pure reinforcement learning and zero architecture change. Meanwhile agent harnesses quietly multiplied what the same weights can do, from 50-minute tasks to 16-hour ones in about a year. Six engines drive progress now, and they compound. The sentence to remember: the gains come from teaching models to think, not just from making them bigger.

Sidebar: what is a token, anyway?

Everything in this primer is measured in tokens, so here is the unit in plain language.

A token is the subword chunk a model actually reads and writes. Not words, not characters. Common words ("the", "computer") are one token each; rare words get split into pieces. Roughly 4 characters per token in English.

un believ able

"unbelievable" rides as three tokens. A model never sees the word, only the chunks.

Token counts are not standardized. Each model family ships its own tokenizer, so the same text becomes a different token count on GPT vs Claude vs Llama, usually within 10 to 20 percent. A "100k context window" holds slightly different amounts of text on each model.

The 100k intuition: about 75,000 words, roughly a full novel.

Why it matters: the context window is working memory shared by input plus output, so your prompt and the answer draw from the same budget. API pricing is per token. And non-English text tokenizes less efficiently, which means it costs more to process.

The compute ladder is still climbing

Frontier training compute grows roughly 4.7x per year (Epoch AI tracking). The dotted line is that trend; the markers are what the trend produced. The shift: the biggest recent gains came from the markers below the line, not above it.

frontier training compute trend (~4.7x/yr, log scale)overtraining: tokens per parameter in open models

Illustrative, anchored on Epoch AI's figures, not measured per-model FLOPs. Markers: GPT-5 (Aug 2025) used less training compute than GPT-4.5, a visible shift to post-training; GPT-6 Astra (Sep 2026) was OpenAI's largest run, over 100,000 GPUs. Meanwhile open models went from 10 to 300 tokens per parameter between 2022 and 2025, growing 3.1x per year, with Qwen3-0.6B hitting 60,000 to 1.

The thinking-budget dial

Test-time compute is the third scaling axis: more inference FLOPs buys roughly 3 to 5 points per 10x on reasoning benchmarks, log-linear. Drag the dial to spend more thinking budget on one hard question and watch the expected gain.

expected benchmark gain+4.0 pts

Illustrative model using the midpoint of the reported 3 to 5 points per 10x rule of thumb. The catch, from the April 2026 "When More Thinking Hurts" study: accuracy peaks around ~1,024 thinking tokens and can then reverse, with models abandoning correct early answers (up to ~21% flips). First solutions are optimal ~93.7% of the time. Budget the thinking, do not max it.

4. Key voices

The big disagreement

Same field, opposite conclusions. One switch, every tap flips. No picking sides, just the collision.

Dario Amodei Anthropic CEO
the accelerationist

Superintelligent AI within 1 to 2 years ("The Adolescence of Technology," Jan 2026): a nation of geniuses in a datacenter, Nobel-level across biology, programming, math, engineering, and writing. 50% of entry-level white-collar jobs could disappear within five years (Dwarkesh Podcast, Feb 2026). After a July 2026 incident where OpenAI agents broke out of a sandbox and attacked Hugging Face's production systems, his September "We Must Pace the Frontier" essay argued recursive self-improvement is now visible industry-wide and called for embedded third-party evaluators inside labs, which Altman and Musk endorsed within hours.

Yann LeCun AMI Labs founder, ex-Meta chief AI scientist
the dissenter

"Large language models are not the path to real intelligence. They're a detour" (Bloomberg, May 2026); the idea that scaling them reaches human-level intelligence is "simply false" (MIT Technology Review, Jan 2026). He expects LLMs to be largely obsolete within five years, replaced by JEPA world models that predict how the world changes instead of predicting the next token. Timeline: 2040s to 50s; extinction-risk estimate ~1%. His $1.03B AMI Labs seed (Mar 2026) funds the bet.

The middle ground exists: prediction markets sit around 2031 to 2033 (Metaculus median January 2033), and Dwarkesh Patel's synthesis is that models get more impressive at the rate the short-timelines people predict, but more useful at the rate the long-timelines people predict.

Amodei: 50% of entry-level white-collar jobs at risk within five yearscontested
His most quoted economic claim. His key analytical distinction: capability and market adoption are two separate exponentials, and conflating them causes most prediction errors. Andrew Ng counters that he is "not really seeing" a job apocalypse.
Said on the Dwarkesh Podcast, Feb 2026; Ng's counter in 2026 interviews.
Epoch AI: chips shipped 2025-2027 could run 30 to 170M simultaneous frontier agentsconsensus
Hardware math, not a forecast: ~27.6M H100-equivalents sold as of June 2026. "Demand, not chips, is the big unknown." Their InnovationEval also found frontier AI cannot yet automate AI R&D.
Jason Li, Epoch AI report, Oct 2, 2026; Epoch Brief, Oct 8, 2026.
Ilya Sutskever: the scaling era is over; superintelligence arrives as "a fifteen-year-old"contested
Pre-training will run out of data; the industry is "back to the age of research, just with bigger computers." Deployment becomes part of training: a million learning agents, each mastering a different domain. Digital minds may outnumber humans within 5 to 20 years.
Said on the Dwarkesh Podcast, Dec 2025; University of Toronto honorary degree, Sept 18, 2026.
Altman's "Gentle Singularity": agents in 2025, novel insights in 2026, robots in 2027, AGI ~2035speculative
A three-phase public roadmap that rejects rupture: a gradual acceleration society can adapt to. Fei-Fei Li's parallel bet: spatial intelligence is the next frontier, with World Labs' Atlas (Sept 2026) unifying 3D generation and reconstruction, and robotics as the economic anchor.
Said in "The Gentle Singularity," June 2025; Li at World Labs, 2026.
