46 pieces of advice from 12 billionaires

October 5, 2026 · business · interviews

The School of Hard Knocks YouTube channel interviews billionaire founders about what actually worked. I collected the sharpest advice from 12 of them — Adam Weitsman, Bill Ackman, Daniel Lubetzky, David Green, Dawn Lafreeda, Forbes Riley, Grant Cardone, Honey co-founder, John Caudwell, Kirt Linington, Marcus Lemonis, Rick Jackson — and organized it into 7 themes.

No fluff, no morning-routine cosplay. Just the operating principles, in their words.

Mindset 12

How they think.

Maximize the risk for the right reasons. Take real risk, but for mission and ministry — not personal income.

David Green · Billionaire founder of Hobby Lobby: $600 loan to ~$10B/year, 1,100+ stores.
School of Hard Knocks — I Started With $600... Now I Make $10B/Year

Fear of failure plus the will to win is an incredible force. Don't fear the fear — channel it.

John Caudwell · British billionaire, founder of Phones 4U (sold for ~$1.5B).
School of Hard Knocks — He Sold His Business for $1.5 BILLION Doing THIS

Scale by working on the business instead of in the business. No investors, no outside money.

Rick Jackson · Founder of Jackson Healthcare ($3B+ revenue, zero outside money); grew up in the projects, foster care at 13.
School of Hard Knocks — He Made $3 BILLION in One Year Doing THIS

I never saw myself as a victim. I don't blame anybody but me. You can't control anybody else — you can only control yourself.

Rick Jackson · Founder of Jackson Healthcare ($3B+ revenue, zero outside money); grew up in the projects, foster care at 13.
School of Hard Knocks — He Made $3 BILLION in One Year Doing THIS

You can't sit in business school and learn how to build a business. Building is learned by doing.

Honey co-founder · Co-founder of Honey (sold to PayPal for $4B); was still broke at 38.
School of Hard Knocks — He's a Tech BILLIONAIRE

If you don't love what you do, then you shouldn't do it. Money is a byproduct, not the purpose.

Marcus Lemonis · Camping World CEO, star of CNBC's The Profit ($900M+).
School of Hard Knocks — He Made Over $900 Million Doing THIS

A man that's too proud to pick up a penny is not worth a penny. Ego is the only thing more powerful than greed — stay humble enough to sweat the small stuff.

Daniel Lubetzky · Founder of KIND Bar (billion-dollar brand).
School of Hard Knocks — Daniel Lubetzky interview (transcript)

Final principle: be kind to every human being.

Daniel Lubetzky · Founder of KIND Bar (billion-dollar brand).
School of Hard Knocks — Daniel Lubetzky interview (transcript)

His dad's advice on the drive to prison: don't be a victim — nobody's coming to save you. You have to save yourself.

Adam Weitsman · Scrap-metal billionaire ($60-70M net/year shredding junk cars).
School of Hard Knocks — Adam Weitsman interview (transcript)

What you know is more important than who you know. The ideas are more valuable than the relationships.

Bill Ackman · Founder of Pershing Square (~$9B).
School of Hard Knocks — Bill Ackman interview (via Moneywise)

There's no such thing as business problems — just personal problems that roll into the business. Fix yourself first.

Kirt Linington · Roofing mogul: came to America with $700, started door-knocking at 42, sold for nine figures.
School of Hard Knocks podcast — Kirt Linington (transcript via Podscan)

I wouldn't let no be an answer. Banks didn't believe a 23-year-old who looked 12 was the owner; she kept forging ahead until she found people to deal with.

Money & Investing 11

How they handle capital.

Chase the deal, not the money. Most people chase debt and equity; chase the right deal, because the right deal brings the money.

Grant Cardone · CEO of Cardone Capital ($2B+ real estate assets), star of Undercover Billionaire.
School of Hard Knocks — Young Entrepreneur Interviews Billionaire Grant Cardone

What the wealthy know that you don't: how to reduce their tax bill. Get a handle on that.

Grant Cardone · CEO of Cardone Capital ($2B+ real estate assets), star of Undercover Billionaire.
School of Hard Knocks — Young Entrepreneur Interviews Billionaire Grant Cardone

The 10% rule: never more than 10% of purchases from one supplier, 10% of sales to one customer, or 10% of salesmanship in one salesman. Learned the hard way when Motorola cut him off and he nearly lost 95% of revenue overnight.

John Caudwell · British billionaire, founder of Phones 4U (sold for ~$1.5B).
School of Hard Knocks — He Sold His Business for $1.5 BILLION Doing THIS

The best companies are bought, they're not sold. Create enough value that someone wants to buy you instead of shopping the company around.

Honey co-founder · Co-founder of Honey (sold to PayPal for $4B); was still broke at 38.
School of Hard Knocks — He's a Tech BILLIONAIRE

Double R stands for recurring revenue. That's all that matters. If you can retain your customers, even if you don't have that many, you can still do well.

Honey co-founder · Co-founder of Honey (sold to PayPal for $4B); was still broke at 38.
School of Hard Knocks — He's a Tech BILLIONAIRE

Know three numbers cold: annual sales revenue, gross profit margins, expenses as a percentage of gross profit. Control your cash, stick to your core business, and know your numbers.

Marcus Lemonis · Camping World CEO, star of CNBC's The Profit ($900M+).
School of Hard Knocks — He Made Over $900 Million Doing THIS

Read the small print — get a magnifying glass before you sign. Banks bury terms; know exactly what you're signing.

Adam Weitsman · Scrap-metal billionaire ($60-70M net/year shredding junk cars).
School of Hard Knocks — Adam Weitsman interview (transcript)

Respect leverage. He started over-leveraged on bank loans — a total mess. Banks are great until you cross them, then they'll bury you.

Adam Weitsman · Scrap-metal billionaire ($60-70M net/year shredding junk cars).
School of Hard Knocks — Adam Weitsman interview (transcript)

If you have a great, creative, brilliant idea, the capital will find you.

Bill Ackman · Founder of Pershing Square (~$9B).
School of Hard Knocks — Bill Ackman interview (via Moneywise)

The biggest challenge when you're young is you want to make money really fast. In investing, that's almost a guaranteed bad outcome.

Bill Ackman · Founder of Pershing Square (~$9B).
School of Hard Knocks — Bill Ackman interview (via Moneywise)

Learn the business, not just the craft. A side job at an accounting-software company taught her payroll and accounting: you may know everything about running a restaurant but nothing about running a business.

Dawn Lafreeda · Denny's largest sole-ownership franchisee: 60+ locations, ~$98M revenue. Started as a hostess at 16.

Habits & Discipline 8

What they do daily.

Her operating rules: surround yourself with the right people; delegate what you're bad at; your network is your net worth — call people when you don't need them so they're there when you do; learn daily; build multiple income streams online.

Forbes Riley · 10-figure infomercial entrepreneur, $2.5B+ in products sold.
School of Hard Knocks — She Sold $2.5 BILLION Worth of Products Doing THIS

The 10X Rule: set targets 10X bigger than you think you can hit, and take 10X the action you think is necessary. The biggest mistake most people make in life is not setting goals high enough.

Grant Cardone · CEO of Cardone Capital ($2B+ real estate assets), star of Undercover Billionaire.
School of Hard Knocks — Young Entrepreneur Interviews Billionaire Grant Cardone

Free time is the devil. Stay in massive action; separate happiness from wealth — seek accomplishment, have fun.

Grant Cardone · CEO of Cardone Capital ($2B+ real estate assets), star of Undercover Billionaire.
School of Hard Knocks — Young Entrepreneur Interviews Billionaire Grant Cardone

Distribution came from literal hard knocks: checking into bodegas one at a time, even pausing dates to pitch stores. Slowly but surely, you just keep building it.

Daniel Lubetzky · Founder of KIND Bar (billion-dollar brand).
School of Hard Knocks — Daniel Lubetzky interview (transcript)

No master plan: hit the streets, knock the doors, get the sales. He started knocking at 42 and exited nine figures.

Kirt Linington · Roofing mogul: came to America with $700, started door-knocking at 42, sold for nine figures.
School of Hard Knocks podcast — Kirt Linington (transcript via Podscan)

Do the unsexy work nobody wants to do anymore. You could create a lane for yourself doing the jobs others avoid.

Kirt Linington · Roofing mogul: came to America with $700, started door-knocking at 42, sold for nine figures.
School of Hard Knocks podcast — Kirt Linington (transcript via Podscan)

Take action before confidence arrives. She bought her first restaurant at 23 with family loans and maxed-out credit cards.

Pitching & Sales 6

How they sell.

People don't buy because you explain something. They buy because you show them what it can do. Demonstrate, don't explain — her biggest sales day came from demoing SpinGym with real people using it: 64,000 units, about $1M.

Forbes Riley · 10-figure infomercial entrepreneur, $2.5B+ in products sold.
School of Hard Knocks — She Sold $2.5 BILLION Worth of Products Doing THIS

Sell the outcome, not the object. She sold over $1B in Jack LaLanne juicers without selling juicers at all — pitch the result, not the product.

Forbes Riley · 10-figure infomercial entrepreneur, $2.5B+ in products sold.
School of Hard Knocks — She Sold $2.5 BILLION Worth of Products Doing THIS

A pitch is a confident explanation of why a product benefits real people in real need — not pushy or sleazy. Lead every pitch with a 15-second emotional story plus fast, concrete proof; the conviction transmits through the screen and that's what closes.

Forbes Riley · 10-figure infomercial entrepreneur, $2.5B+ in products sold.
School of Hard Knocks — She Sold $2.5 BILLION Worth of Products Doing THIS

Really good customer service will deliver sales. Train salesmen to give the best possible advice and then achieve the sale. People actually like you to ask for a sale because it shows you value their business.

John Caudwell · British billionaire, founder of Phones 4U (sold for ~$1.5B).
School of Hard Knocks — He Sold His Business for $1.5 BILLION Doing THIS

His #1 sales and negotiation rule: you can have everything in the world you want if you help other people get what they want. The more money you make other people, the more money you're going to make yourself.

Rick Jackson · Founder of Jackson Healthcare ($3B+ revenue, zero outside money); grew up in the projects, foster care at 13.
School of Hard Knocks — He Made $3 BILLION in One Year Doing THIS

On branding: what's your speed? Consistent. A brand is a promise, well kept — decide what the brand stands for AND what it is not; don't try to please everybody.

Daniel Lubetzky · Founder of KIND Bar (billion-dollar brand).
School of Hard Knocks — Daniel Lubetzky interview (transcript)

Failure & Resilience 4

What losing taught them.

Started making tiny picture frames in a garage; nearly lost everything in the mid-80s oil bust — literally crawled under his desk and cried out to God — then kept going and stayed consistent.

David Green · Billionaire founder of Hobby Lobby: $600 loan to ~$10B/year, 1,100+ stores.
School of Hard Knocks — I Started With $600... Now I Make $10B/Year

Failure is the fastest way to get where you want to go — admit mistakes, analyze, apply. He publicly owned a bad executive hire: by owning the problem, you also own the ability to influence the situation.

Rick Jackson · Founder of Jackson Healthcare ($3B+ revenue, zero outside money); grew up in the projects, foster care at 13.
School of Hard Knocks — He Made $3 BILLION in One Year Doing THIS

Building a business sometimes feels like hell. The only way through hell is through it.

Honey co-founder · Co-founder of Honey (sold to PayPal for $4B); was still broke at 38.
School of Hard Knocks — He's a Tech BILLIONAIRE

On entrepreneurship: it's tough, you don't sleep much, it's not that sexy. Go in clear-eyed.

Adam Weitsman · Scrap-metal billionaire ($60-70M net/year shredding junk cars).
School of Hard Knocks — Adam Weitsman interview (transcript)

Hiring & Team 3

Who they build with.

Keep tight control of costs, but pay people very well and expect them to deliver results. If you can't make it work by paying well, get out of the industry.

John Caudwell · British billionaire, founder of Phones 4U (sold for ~$1.5B).
School of Hard Knocks — He Sold His Business for $1.5 BILLION Doing THIS

The 3 P's: People (right people in the right roles — no more, no less), Process (minimize waste; time is money), Product (exceptional and unique). If one is broken, fix it.

Marcus Lemonis · Camping World CEO, star of CNBC's The Profit ($900M+).
School of Hard Knocks — He Made Over $900 Million Doing THIS

On partners: if you can't communicate as business partners, you can't be in business together. The best deals are when both parties come to an agreement and feel just a little uncomfortable.

Marcus Lemonis · Camping World CEO, star of CNBC's The Profit ($900M+).
School of Hard Knocks — He Made Over $900 Million Doing THIS

Faith & Stewardship 2

What they believe the money is for.

Give the true owner the vote. Before any big decision, check it against what God would agree with — throw out a number on a deal that's a miracle if it works, so you know it's not just you deciding.

David Green · Billionaire founder of Hobby Lobby: $600 loan to ~$10B/year, 1,100+ stores.
School of Hard Knocks — I Started With $600... Now I Make $10B/Year

Build for 150 years, not just the next generation. He moved 100% of Hobby Lobby's voting stock into a stewardship trust: not set up so they can sell it and sit on the beach.

David Green · Billionaire founder of Hobby Lobby: $600 loan to ~$10B/year, 1,100+ stores.
