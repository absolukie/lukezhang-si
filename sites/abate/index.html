FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this legal or compliance advice?

No. Abate assembles your records; it doesn't interpret regulations. Confirm every requirement with your accredited AHERA/OSHA program.

Does it work without cell service on site?

Yes. Everything is stored on the device as the crew works. The dossier assembles locally: no connection needed until you print or export.

Who owns our data?

You do. Records live in your browser, and the archive export is a plain file you keep. Your records stay on this device unless you sign in or join a shared device key, which uploads encrypted copies to our sync server.

What happens at 100% completeness?

100% means every required record is present and evidenced. That is the complete file you hand the auditor, the insurer, or the property buyer.

Can pass/fail on air samples be overridden?

No, and that's the point. Limits are encoded from OSHA PELs and computed automatically, so the record is defensible.

Stop rebuilding the file the night before the audit.

Log it as it happens. The dossier builds itself.
