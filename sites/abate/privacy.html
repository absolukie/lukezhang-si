Privacy

Last updated October 9, 2026. Plain words, no fine print.

Where your records live

Everything you enter in Abate, daily logs, air samples, manifests, photos, toolbox talks, sign-offs, is stored in your own browser on your own device. Nothing leaves the device unless you turn on device sync (see below) or export the archive yourself.

Device sync (optional prototype)

If you sign in or join a shared device key, copies of your records upload to our sync server so a second device can see the same file. Photo uploads carry metadata and a small thumbnail only; the full-resolution photo never leaves the device that took it. Sync is off until you set it up.

Billing

Paid plans are handled by Stripe through our billing server. Card numbers go to Stripe, never to us, and never into the app.

What we do not do

  • No advertising in the app, and no sale of your records to anyone.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs in the app. The app makes no third-party network requests.
  • We cannot see records that stay on your device.

Your control

Your records are yours. Export the archive any time from the Dossier tab; it is a plain file you keep. Deleting a project in the app removes it from that device. Records already synced to the server under your device key stay on the server until you ask us to remove them: write to absolukie@gmail.com.

A note on scope

This page describes the Abate prototype as it works today. Terms of service and a data-processing addendum for paid company plans will be added before general availability, and will be reviewed with counsel first.
