FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need internet on the job site?

No. Everything is stored on the device as the crew works. The dossier generates locally — no connection needed until you print or send the PDF.

Who owns our job data?

You do. Records live in your browser; CSV exports are plain files you keep. Device sync is on by default: copies are stored on our server so your other devices can read them.

Will adjusters accept this format?

The dossier is built around what adjusters ask for: timestamped before/during/after photos, a completed decontamination checklist, line-item billing, and a chain-of-custody log — with signature lines.

What does the dossier score mean?

It's the share of the adjuster's checklist your file actually satisfies: claim details, photo evidence, completed checklist, work record. At 90%+, the file is ready to send without a fight.

Is this legal advice?

No. Aftermath documents conditions and work performed. It doesn't interpret policy language or regulations.
