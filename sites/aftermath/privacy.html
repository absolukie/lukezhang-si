Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: your job data stays on your device. Aftermath is a local-first app. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, no account system, and we do not sell your data. The exceptions are listed below, in plain language.

What lives on your device

Everything you enter is stored in your browser's local storage, on your device only (about a 5 MB ceiling, which the app's storage meter shows you):

  • Job details: job names, client names, property addresses, claim numbers, insurer names, adjuster names and phone numbers, loss descriptions.
  • Photos: downscaled in your browser to a 1400px JPEG plus a small thumbnail. The original full-resolution photo never leaves your device.
  • Photo metadata: room, damage type, severity, phase, notes, markers, and best-effort GPS coordinates captured at import (approximate; if you deny location permission, no GPS is stored and the import still works).
  • Work records: checklist checks, line items, custody log events, technician and client signatures.

Device sync (prototype)

Aftermath includes an experimental sync feature that copies photo thumbnails and record metadata to our prototype server (sync-proto.lukezhang.si) so your other devices can read the same jobs. Full-resolution photos never upload. Sync is identified by a random per-device key stored on your device; there is no account and no password. Be aware this is prototype-grade: anyone holding that device key could read and write this device's synced records. A paying pilot runs local-only with exports until the sync backend is rebuilt.

Billing

Billing is currently in test mode. The 14-day trial requires no card, and no payment details are collected inside the app. Trial status is checked against our test server; if that check fails, the app keeps working (it fails open) and the failure is only logged in the browser console.

What we do not do

  • No analytics SDK ships with the app. We do not track how you use it.
  • No advertising, no ad trackers, no cross-site tracking.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • The demo job uses clearly labeled sample photos; nothing about the demo leaves your device either.

Device permissions

The app asks for the camera only when you add photos, and for location only to attach approximate coordinates to photos. Both are optional: the file picker works without the camera, and denying location simply stores no GPS. Permissions are used only for the features described above.

Deleting your data

Reset all data at the bottom of the jobs list wipes everything on the device. Clearing the site's data in your browser settings does the same. To ask about copies on the prototype sync server, contact us below.
