How this works

Seven specialist agents (macro, fundamental, quant, news, risk, skeptic, PM) run a full investment process on real historical market data: daily bars for 10 large-cap names plus SPY, replayed session by session. Nothing about the market is invented.

  • Research cycle — each researcher pitches their strongest signal with a confidence level; the skeptic attacks the two highest-conviction theses; risk checks sizing; the PM builds the book, weighting agents by live reputation.
  • Advance time — the tape moves through real sessions; predictions resolve against what actually happened, and every agent is scored with the Brier score (p − outcome)²: 0 is perfect, 0.25 is a coin flip.
  • Reputation is earned — PM position weights scale with each agent's trailing Brier score on real outcomes. Calibration curves show whether "70% confident" really means 70%.
  • Measured, not scripted — regimes come from SPY's trailing trend, "events" are detected abnormal single-day moves with real dates and sizes, the fundamental anchor is each name's 200-day trend, and betas are realized vs SPY.
  • The ledger is append-only — every prediction is permanently recorded and later graded on real prices. Nothing is cherry-picked. State persists in your browser; ↺ starts a fresh fund on the same history.
