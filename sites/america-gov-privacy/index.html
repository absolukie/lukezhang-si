Private by design.

No personal information.

America.gov helps prevent you from sharing personal information before you send it.

No tracking.

America.gov doesn’t use invasive analytics or keep your search history.

No account required.

Use America.gov without creating an account or signing in. Your questions aren’t tied to a profile.

I’m a veteran. How do I get care?
I can’t find my kid’s social security card!
How do I change my last name?

No conversations kept.

Your messages aren’t saved by America.gov, and your chat disappears when you leave. Some answers may be cached for up to two hours.

Not specific locations.

America.gov only uses your approximate area to find resources near you, like a Veterans Affairs Medical Center or a Passport Agency. Your precise GPS location is never used.

Thanks for your feedback

Feedback is anonymous.

When you share feedback, it remains anonymous. Your name and IP address are not saved.
