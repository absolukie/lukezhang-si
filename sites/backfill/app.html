  2. Waitlist gets texted, in priority order, seconds after the cancel.
  3. First YES wins, the slot fills, the calendar updates, revenue is recovered.

Why this matters

Dental practices lose 10-15% of appointments to cancellations and no-shows. At $150-400 per chair hour, one unfilled cancellation a day is $3,000-8,000 a month walking out the door. Backfill's job: turn a cancellation into a filled slot in minutes, not hours of phone tag.
