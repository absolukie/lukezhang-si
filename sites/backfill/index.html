Questions

Straight answers.

Does it work with my scheduling software?
The production version connects to the major dental systems (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental) and reads cancellations as they happen. The demo on this page is a simulation with sample data.
Is patient data safe?
Yes. The production product is HIPAA compliant with signed BAAs, encrypted messaging, and opt-in consent for every patient. The demo uses fictional patients and sends nothing.
What if nobody on the waitlist says yes?
Backfill tries the top of the list first, then works down. If the slot stays open, your front desk gets a nudge with the remaining candidates and their phone numbers. You lose nothing by trying.
Won't patients find the texts annoying?
Only waitlisted patients who asked for earlier slots get texts, and only when a matching slot opens. Reply STOP opts out instantly. Practices report patients love it.
How fast does it actually fill slots?
In the demo, about 4 minutes from cancellation to confirmation. In production it depends on your waitlist depth, but the first text goes out within 30 seconds of the cancellation.
