Privacy policy

What Backfill does with your data

Backfill is a demo for dental front desks. The rule is simple: everything you type stays on your device, and the demo never handles real patient data.

What is stored on your device

  • Waitlist entries you add: the name you type, needed treatment, contact preference, availability, language, and short-notice preference. Stored in your browser's local storage, on this device only.
  • Schedule edits, cancelled and filled slots, the activity log, and recall campaign results. Same place: local storage, this device only.
  • Draft text you typed into the waitlist form, kept until you submit it or it is cleared.

What is synced to a server

If device sync is on, a copy of the data above goes to our sync prototype so it can follow you across devices. The server identifies your device with a random key, not with your name or your patients' names. It is a prototype: the last change wins, and access cannot be revoked yet. It is not the compliance path for real patient data.

What never happens

  • No real patient names, phone numbers, or clinical notes. The demo ships with fictional patients, and every simulated send is labeled as simulated.
  • No texts are actually sent. The outreach you see is theater.
  • No sale of data, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking cookies in this demo.

Billing

When billing is switched on, card details go directly to Stripe. We never see or store card numbers. The demo currently runs in Stripe test mode, so no real charge can happen.

Deletion

Tap Reset demo in the Schedule view to wipe the demo dataset from this device. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything else Backfill stored.
