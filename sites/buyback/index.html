Questions people ask

Is this legal advice?

No. Buyback is an organizational tool, not a law firm. Lemon law is fact-specific and state-specific. Before you act, talk to a licensed lemon law attorney in your state. Most offer free case reviews and work on contingency.

What counts as the "same problem"?

Use the same short label for the same symptom every visit, for example "transmission shudder." Different dealers count together as long as the complaint is the same. What matters is the recurring defect, not where you took it.

Do days in the shop have to be consecutive?

No. In all fifteen states the day counts are cumulative across visits. Keep every repair order: the date-in and date-out on each invoice is what proves your total.

What if my car is used?

Some states cover used cars still under the manufacturer's original warranty (California does, for example). Texas generally covers new vehicles only. The tool tracks the presumption windows; an attorney can tell you if your used car qualifies another way.

How is this free? How do you make money?

Buyback is free for consumers, and it stays free. If you choose to get matched with a lemon law attorney through the case-review form, participating attorneys may pay us a referral fee. That never changes what you pay: lemon law attorneys work on contingency, so there is no upfront cost to you either way.

Where does my data go?

On your devices: everything you enter is stored in your browser's local storage, and device sync keeps copies on our server so your other devices can read them. There are no accounts and no tracking.
