12-volt battery repeatedly dies, car goes fully dead

A 2025 class action alleges the 12-volt accessory battery in 2023-2025 bZ4X models repeatedly dies, leaving the car completely dead even when the main battery shows 77 percent charge. One plaintiff's car needed three replacements within 5,000 miles, and owners report the car going dead after sitting overnight or at airports. The suit claims Toyota and Subaru knew of the problem before the cars went on sale.

HVAC and defroster software fault

A software fault in the HVAC control unit can disable the defroster and defogger after certain compressor failures, destroying visibility in cold or humid conditions. Toyota recalled roughly 94,000 Toyota, Lexus, and Subaru EVs for this defect. Owners also report weak heat output in very cold weather beyond the recall scope.

Slow fast charging and charging glitches

Owners consistently report DC fast charging that never exceeds about 60 kW, far below rivals that charge above 150 kW, turning road trips into long waits. Some home chargers and public Level 2 stations fail to initiate or complete charging, and charge-port door problems add frustration. Survey data shows charging glitches across both home and public networks.

Severe cold-weather range loss

Colorado owners in buyback proceedings report 30 percent less range at 25 degrees, dropping real-world range toward 160 miles against an advertised 228-plus. Independent testing has shown range as low as 160 miles, and highway driving worsens the drop. Some owners describe the advertised range as a complete miss and are paying lease penalties just to get rid of the car.

Rearview camera freezing or blank

Some 2022-2026 Toyota and Lexus models including the bZ4X are covered by a recall for a rearview camera image that can freeze or go blank due to software errors in the panoramic view system. A frozen backup camera removes a key safety aid when reversing. The fix is a software update at the dealer.

Toyota bZ4X lemon questions

Is my Toyota bZ4X a lemon?

Maybe. It depends on your state's thresholds and your repair history, not on the defect alone. If your bZ4X has been in for the same problem 3 or more times, or has spent weeks in the shop, log every visit in the tracker and let it do the counting against your state's statute.

What should I do after each dealer visit?

Get the repair order before you leave, check that the complaint is written the way you described it, and note the date in, date out, and mileage. Photograph the invoice and log the visit the same day. Gaps in paperwork are how winnable cases get lost.

Do the repair attempts have to be for the same problem?

For the repeat-repair routes, generally yes: the same defect, attempted and failed multiple times. The days-out-of-service route is different, it is cumulative across all warranty defects. The tracker counts both separately so you can see every path.

Should I talk to a lawyer now or keep waiting?

Lemon law attorneys offer free case reviews and work on contingency, so an early conversation costs nothing. But whatever you decide, keep logging: evidence you collect now is what makes a case review turn into a case.
