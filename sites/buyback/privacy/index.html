Privacy policy

Your case data lives on your devices.

Last updated October 9, 2026. The short version: everything you enter is stored in your browser first. Your device also keeps backup copies on our sync server so your other devices can read them. No tracking, no ads, no selling your data.

What stays on your device

Your state, car details, repair visits, checklist progress, estimates, and recall results are stored in your browser's local storage on that device. Nothing leaves the device except through device sync or a case review request, described below.

Device sync (prototype)

Your device registers itself with our sync service automatically and uploads copies of your case (your state, car, repairs, and any case review snapshot) over an encrypted connection, so your other devices can pull them down. Sync identifies your devices with a random device key kept in your browser; anyone holding that key can read the synced records, so treat it like a password and only paste it into your own devices. Repair-order photos never leave the device they were taken on.

There is no off switch for sync in this prototype, and sync is a convenience backup, not a permanent evidence vault: keep your paper repair orders. If you want your server copies deleted, email us (see below).

Case review requests

If you tap "Request free case review," the name, phone number, and email you enter, plus a frozen snapshot of your case (state, car, repairs, and threshold results), are saved on your device and shared with a participating lemon law attorney so they can review your file. Repair-order photos are never shared. If you are matched, the participating attorney may pay us a referral fee; that never changes what you pay. No attorney-client relationship forms until you sign an agreement with a firm.

What we never do

No tracking, no analytics cookies, no advertising, and no data brokers. We do not sell your personal information, and we share it with no one except as described on this page.

Deleting your data

"Delete my case data" wipes your car, repairs, and everything stored in the browser on that device. If you used device sync, backup copies also exist on our sync server; email absolukie@gmail.com and we will delete them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about your data: absolukie@gmail.com.
