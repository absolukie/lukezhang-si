    Membership

    Every game is free, forever. Membership is for people who want the club around it — early builds, a vote on what ships next, and something physical in the mail once a year.

    Why charge at all if the games are free?

    Because the club is the product. Members fund the studio directly, so the games never need ads, paywalls, or dark patterns. Free players get the full games; members get the backstage.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes, in one tap, and you keep member perks until the end of the billing period. The cancel button is not hidden in a maze. That's a brand rule.

    What does the yearly gift look like?

    Something small and physical from the studio — a print, a pin, a zine about how a game got made. Different every year, always real.

    How we build

    Who's behind the studio?

    Luke — an indie maker who builds small digital products for fun and, increasingly, for a living. One person, no investors, no roadmap theater.

    Can I suggest a game?

    Members vote on what ships next every quarter. The winning idea gets built, and the suggester gets credited in the game.

    Do you do client work?

    No. The studio only ships its own products. That's the whole point — and why everything feels like it was made by someone who cared.
