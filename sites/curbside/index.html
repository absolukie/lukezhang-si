Questions

Does Curbside replace my POS like Square or Toast?

No. Curbside is the operator layer, not the register. Keep your POS; Curbside handles everything around it: permits, spots, commissary, events, and the money picture.

Which cities are covered?

Los Angeles, Austin, Portland, Denver, Chicago, Miami, New York, San Francisco, Seattle, Houston, Atlanta, and Nashville, with real permit names, fees, and renewal cycles researched for each. More cities get added as owners request them.

Is this legal advice?

No. Fees are typical and rules change; always verify with the issuing agency. Curbside keeps you organized and reminds you, it doesn't replace the agency's word.

Does it work on my phone?

That's the whole point. Curbside is built phone-first for the lot, the commissary, and the window. The app keeps working offline once loaded, and your data stays on your device.

Where does my data live?

In your browser, on your device. Device sync is an experimental prototype that starts on its own and has no on/off switch; it is not a backup. Use Export backup in Settings for a real copy of your data. Your business data is yours; nothing is sold, ever.

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days with full access, no credit card required. When the trial ends, you pick a plan to keep going. Your data stays on your device the whole time, and you can export it anytime.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly billing, no contract, no cancellation maze.
