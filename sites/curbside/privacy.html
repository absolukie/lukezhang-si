Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

Your truck data stays on your device

Curbside stores your permits, expiry dates, spot schedule, events, revenue, commissary details, and checklist state in your browser's local storage, on your own phone or computer. The app works fully offline from that data.

The JSON backup you export is a file you download. It leaves your device only if you move it somewhere yourself.

Device sync is a prototype

To keep your truck data available on your other devices, the app registers a random per-device key (no name, no email, no account needed) and sends copies of your records to our sync server at sync-proto.lukezhang.si. Anyone who holds your device key can read that data, so treat the key like a password.

Records over 100 KB stay on the device that created them and are never uploaded. Sync is a convenience, not a backup: the JSON export is the safety net.

Sign-in is optional

Curbside works fully without an account. If you sign in, it links this device to you so sync spans your devices. Three ways in:

Email magic link: we collect your email address to send the sign-in link (sent through our backend's email provider). Phone code: we collect your phone number to text the code (sent through our backend's SMS provider). Sign in with Apple or Google: Apple or Google handle the login step and share your account identifier with our backend under their own privacy policies.

Sign-in data is never sold and is used only to keep your devices in sync.

Payments are in test mode

Curbside billing is currently in Stripe test mode: no real charges happen. Trial status, card setup, and the customer portal go through our backend to Stripe; Curbside never sees or stores your card details. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to the payment step.

Crash reports are scrubbed first

If the app hits an error, it may send a short error report to our server so crashes can be fixed. Email addresses, phone numbers, and keys are redacted on your device before anything is sent. No truck data is included in these reports.

Phone line

If you get a text or voice tier, your truck gets its own local phone number. The truck name, menu text, hours, location text, and owner mobile you set in Settings are stored on our backend so the text auto-replies (menu, location, hours, orders) have something to say. Your owner mobile is where order and call alerts go; customers never see it.

The number itself is leased through Twilio, which carries the calls and texts under Twilio's own privacy policy.

What we never do

No ads. No analytics trackers. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the services named above: the sync server, Stripe for payments, Twilio for the phone line, Apple and Google if you sign in with them, and the email/SMS providers our backend uses to send sign-in links and codes.
