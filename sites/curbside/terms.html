1. What Curbside does

Curbside is a phone-first app for food truck owners. It keeps your permits, spot schedule, events, revenue, and commissary details in one place, and it gives your truck its own local phone number so customers can text you for your menu, location, hours, and pickup orders. Some plans add an AI agent that answers calls for you.

2. Your free trial

You get a 14-day free trial with full features. No credit card is required to start the trial.

The trial ends on its own after 14 days. You are never charged automatically from a trial, because we never asked for your card. When the trial ends, the app shows a paywall. Your data is untouched and you can still export it.

3. Paid plans and billing

Plans are billed monthly, in advance, in US dollars. Prices exclude any taxes that may apply where you operate. Plans renew automatically each month until you cancel.

  • Solo, $50/month. One truck. Includes the text tier: your own local phone number with text auto-replies for menu, location, hours, and pickup orders, plus SMS renewal reminders and broadcast location texts.
  • Fleet, $80/month. Two to three trucks on one login. This plan is coming soon and is not available yet.
  • Voice tier. An optional add-on where an AI agent answers your truck's calls, takes pickup orders, and captures catering inquiries after hours. It costs $100/month. Adding or removing it does not change your base plan.

You add a card through Stripe. Curbside never sees or stores your card number. We may change prices in the future, but we will tell you at least 30 days before a change takes effect, and you can cancel before then if you do not want the new price.

4. Phone and text terms

This section covers the phone line and texting, for both you (the truck owner) and your customers.

  • You agree to receive texts from us. By adding a phone tier, you give Curbside express consent to send texts to the owner mobile number you provide. These include order alerts, call and voicemail digests, renewal reminders, and account notices. This consent is not a condition of buying the app.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier may charge you for texts you receive. Check your plan if you are unsure.
  • Reply STOP to opt out. You can reply STOP to any message from us to stop further texts. STOP requests are handled automatically.
  • Your customers and STOP. If you send broadcast texts to your own customer list, you are responsible for having their permission first, and for honoring their opt-outs. Our system processes STOP replies for your truck's number automatically.
  • Carrier filtering. Our texts go out over registered business messaging (A2P 10DLC) through Twilio, which carries the calls and texts. Carriers use spam filters, and some messages may be delayed or blocked. We cannot guarantee delivery of any text.
  • Calls are answered by an AI agent, not a person. On the voice tier, incoming calls are answered by an automated system. Callers are not speaking to a human unless the agent connects them to you. Voicemails arrive to you as transcribed text digests.
  • The number is leased. Your truck's phone number is leased through Twilio. If you stop paying for the phone tier, the number may be released back to the carrier. You can ask about porting a number in or out by emailing us.

5. Your data

Your truck data is yours. We process it only to run the service: keeping the app working, syncing between your devices if you turn sync on, billing through Stripe, and operating your phone line through Twilio. We do not sell your data. Our Privacy Policy lists exactly what we collect and who handles it.

Most of your data lives in your browser's local storage, on your own phone or computer. Sync is a convenience, not a backup. The JSON backup you export is a file you download, and it leaves your device only if you move it yourself.

6. What Curbside is not

Curbside helps you organize your permits. It does not issue permits, approve permits, or speak for any agency. Nothing in the app is a certification, approval, sign-off, or guarantee about your permits. Permit fees and rules come from our researched catalog and are labeled as estimates. Agencies change their rules, so always verify fees, deadlines, and requirements with the issuing agency before you act.

Permit information in Curbside is organizational help, not legal advice. Revenue figures in the app are your own records, not tax documents.
