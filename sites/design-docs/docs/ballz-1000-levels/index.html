Theme 1: TUTORIAL HELL

Levels 1–100 · Electric blue/cyan — the current look. The fundamentals.

  • L1 Standard — the baseline block.
  • L10 Chip — jagged weakness bite; strike it to break instantly.
  • L20 Dense — 2× HP for its level.
  • L30 Brittle — half HP, +50% score.
  • L40 Splitter — breaks into two half-HP minis.
  • L50 Armored — ignores the first hit of each volley.
  • L60 Twin — spawns in pairs; killing one deals 5 damage to the other.
  • L70 Heavy — 3× HP, 3× score.
  • L80 Scorer — bonus score when destroyed.
  • L90 Seeder — drops a +1 ball pickup when destroyed.

Theme 2: BOOMTOWN

Levels 101–200 · Orange/red magma. Death is contagious — everything keys off explosions.

  • L101 Cinder — theme baseline.
  • L110 Bomber — explodes on death, 8 damage to all 8 neighbors.
  • L120 Chainer — on death deals 5 damage to every block of the same type.
  • L130 Volatile — 3-volley fuse; explodes for 10 neighbor damage if not killed in time.
  • L140 Kindling — takes double damage from explosions.
  • L150 Pyre — drops 2 pickups if killed by an explosion.
  • L160 Slag — on death the 4 orthogonal neighbors lose 3 HP.
  • L170 Inferno — big bomber, 15 neighbor damage, 2× HP.
  • L180 Phoenix — respawns once next volley at half HP.
  • L190 Wildfire — if killed by an explosion, its own explosion is +50% bigger.

Theme 3: WIGGLE ROOM

Levels 201–300 · Teal/aqua. Blocks that move.

  • L201 Floater — theme baseline.
  • L210 Drifter — slides one cell left/right each volley, bounces at edges.
  • L220 Dropper — descends an extra cell every 3 volleys.
  • L230 Surfer — shifts one cell toward the launch x each volley.
  • L240 Ebb — only solid on even volleys, balls pass through on odd.
  • L250 Whirlpool — curves nearby balls slightly toward it.
  • L260 Coral — grows +2 HP each volley.
  • L270 Riptide — balls rebound 30% faster off it.
  • L280 Deep — 2×2 mega block with a shared HP pool.
  • L290 Tsunami — every 5 volleys all Tide blocks shift down an extra row.

Theme 4: GASLIGHT

Levels 301–400 · Purple/violet. Unreality.

  • L301 Wisp — theme baseline, slightly translucent.
  • L310 Ghost — only solid every other volley.
  • L320 Mirage — fake block — 1 HP, shatters on touch, no score.
  • L330 Phaser — teleports to a random free cell every 2 volleys.
  • L340 Echo — when hit, spawns a 1-HP echo copy adjacent (once).
  • L350 Veil — invisible until a ball passes within 2 cells.
  • L360 Haunt — on death gives a random neighbor +5 HP.
  • L370 Doppel — copies the HP of the strongest block on screen.
  • L380 Poltergeist — swaps position with a random block each volley.
  • L390 Revenant — destroyed blocks have 25% chance to return as 1-HP wisps.

Theme 5: TOUCH GRASS

Levels 401–500 · Green/earth. Growth and roots.

  • L401 Soil — theme baseline.
  • L410 Regen — heals 3 HP each volley.
  • L420 Rooted — immune to explosion splash damage.
  • L430 Spreader — each volley, adjacent empty cells may sprout 5-HP sprouts.
  • L440 Bloom — on death heals neighboring blocks 5 HP (kill it last).
  • L450 Briar — balls that touch it slow to 50% for 3s.
  • L460 Petrified — gains +10 HP for every 4 volleys it goes unhit.
  • L470 Vine — all Vines share one HP pool.
  • L480 Ancient — very high HP; each hit also deals 2 to a random other block.
  • L490 Worldroot — 3 cells wide, drops 3 pickups on death.

Theme 6: ZOOMIES

Levels 501–600 · Yellow/electric. Ball interaction.

  • L501 Cell — theme baseline.
  • L510 Bouncy — balls rebound 30% faster.
  • L520 Prism — splits a hitting ball into two at 60% speed (once per ball per volley).
  • L530 Magnet — curves balls toward it within 3 cells.
  • L540 Charger — balls touching it gain +25% speed for the volley.
  • L550 Short — on hit, 20% chance to zap itself and deal 5 to a random block.
  • L560 Capacitor — every 5th hit releases a shockwave for 5 to adjacent blocks.
  • L570 Arc — hitting it zaps the nearest block for 3.
  • L580 Overload — on death all balls get +50% speed for the rest of the volley.
  • L590 Storm — every 3 volleys zaps a random block for 8.

Theme 7: BRAIN FREEZE

Levels 601–700 · Ice blue/white. Slowing and freezing.

  • L601 Ice — theme baseline.
  • L610 Glacier — 3× HP and no weakness chip.
  • L620 Tar — balls touching it slow to 50% for 3s.
  • L630 Shiver — aura slows balls within 2 cells by 20%.
  • L640 Snowball — gains +1 HP each volley it goes unhit.
  • L650 Cryo — 30% chance on hit to freeze the ball in place for 1s.
  • L660 Hail — on death spawns three 1-HP shards adjacent.
  • L670 Blizzard — every 4 volleys all balls slow 30% for one volley.
  • L680 Absolute — only balls moving faster than 800 damage it.
  • L690 Thaw — on death speeds all balls +20% for the volley.

Theme 8: SKILL ISSUE

Levels 701–800 · Magenta/crimson. Chip mastery.

  • L701 Crux — theme baseline.
  • L710 Double Chip — two chips, either breaks it.
  • L720 Moving Chip — the chip migrates along edges every volley.
  • L730 Tiny Chip — half-size chip, harder to hit.
  • L740 Golden Chip — chip strike also grants +1 ball next volley.
  • L750 Chipshift — the chip jumps to the opposite edge each time the block is hit.
  • L760 Glassjaw — chip spans a whole edge, but 3× HP.
  • L770 Veiled Chip — chip hidden (no gold marker) until the block loses half HP.
  • L780 Echo Chip — striking the chip gives a random chipless block a chip.
  • L790 Heart — chip pulses; strike during the pulse = instant break, else 5 damage.

Theme 9: ANXIETY

Levels 801–900 · Deep dark blue-black. Pressure and dread.

  • L801 Dark — theme baseline.
  • L810 Timer — 5-volley fuse; if still alive it hardens to 99 HP.
  • L820 Locked — invulnerable until all adjacent blocks are destroyed.
  • L830 Devourer — consumes pickups it touches (no +1 for you).
  • L840 Hunger — gains the HP of any block destroyed adjacent to it.
  • L850 Null — destroys the first ball that touches it each volley.
  • L860 Dread — while alive the danger line counts as one row closer.
  • L870 Nightmare — on death spawns two 3-volley Timer blocks.
  • L880 Abyssal — only chip strikes damage it.
  • L890 Harbinger — every 3 volleys spawns a Locked block nearby.

Theme 10: FEVER DREAM

Levels 901–1000 · Iridescent rainbow. Mastery remixes.

  • L901 Prism — theme baseline, shimmering.
  • L910 Golden — rare, 1 HP, 10× score.
  • L920 Apex Splitter — splitter that also bombs.
  • L930 Apex Drifter — drifter that phases.
  • L940 Apex Regen — regen with armor.
  • L950 Apex Prism — prism that magnetizes.
  • L960 Apex Frost — glacier with a timer.
  • L970 Apex Crux — double moving chip.
  • L980 Apex Void — null that teleports.
  • L990 THE CORE — the level-1000 boss — massive HP, four chips, cycles theme abilities.
