Shipped features

Everything currently live in the lab, as of 2026-10-04.

  • 3 fling feels — Snappy (200ms, straight off), Buttery (320ms spring, slight arc), Floaty (500ms, big rotation).
  • Spammable ✕ / ♥ buttons — decisions commit instantly, no input lock; every tap registers, even mid-fling.
  • KEEP / SKIP stamps flash on the card for button and keyboard decisions, not just drags.
  • Drag with flick detection — a fast flick counts as a decision even under the 120px distance threshold.
  • Undo — the card slides back in from the side it exited; ↺ button, always live when there's history.
  • Arrow keys — ← skip, → keep.
  • Progress + kept count with progress bar, persisted in localStorage across sessions.
  • End-of-deck results — kept/skipped stats, the full kept list, copy-to-clipboard.
  • Numbered, batch-tagged cards — #N plus batch letter on every card.
  • iOS hardened — no double-tap zoom while spamming, no text-selection loupe on the emoji buttons, JS press feedback instead of sticky :active, card drag never scrolls the page.
