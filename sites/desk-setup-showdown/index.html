Fine print

  • RAM is the whole game for local AI. Unified memory is shared between CPU and GPU, so a 24GB Mac can hold a ~20B-parameter quantized model, 36GB holds ~30B, 48GB holds ~70B (tight). More agents = more RAM. The chip matters less than the memory.
  • 5K at 27" is the Retina sweet spot. macOS renders 5K at a perfect 2x scale, so text looks printed. 4K at 27" runs at a fractional scale — slightly softer, and some people never notice. If text crispness is your religion, pay for 5K.
  • One cable is real. The CalDigit TS4 carries both monitors, power, ethernet, and peripherals over a single Thunderbolt cable. The 2026 Studio Display even upgraded to Thunderbolt 5. There is also a CalDigit TS5 Plus (Thunderbolt 5, 140W, 10GbE) if you want the newest thing — it costs more and you will not feel the difference.
  • Deals are constant. Amazon and Best Buy run $150–900 off M5 MacBook Pros on rotation, and the Studio Display has dipped to $1,484. If you're not in a hurry, set a price alert and let the discount come to you.
  • Skipping the dock is valid. A MacBook Pro can drive two monitors with two plain USB-C cables. The dock buys you one-cable convenience, ethernet, and ports — not performance.
  • Your iPhone stays the agent remote. Nothing here changes that. The laptop is the engine room; the phone is the steering wheel.
