Dictation, minus the cloud.

This pad transcribes your voice with open-source Whisper running entirely on your phone.

  • Free forever. No account, no API key, no usage fees.
  • Private. Your audio never leaves this device.
  • One download. The model is ~75 MB (Fast) or ~150 MB (Accurate) — grab it on Wi-Fi once.
  • Honest trade-offs. On-phone is slower than cloud dictation, and this can't replace your keyboard's mic — dictate here, then Copy & paste anywhere.
