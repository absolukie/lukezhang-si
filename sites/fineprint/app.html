How it works: FinePrint scans your contract against 30+ known warranty-trap patterns (exclusion language, maintenance requirements, claim caps, arbitration clauses) and highlights each hit in the text. It is pattern matching, not AI and not a lawyer. It will miss cleverly worded traps and occasionally flag harmless text. Always read the exclusions section yourself before signing.

FinePrint is an educational tool, not legal advice. Pattern-based detection cannot catch every trap.
