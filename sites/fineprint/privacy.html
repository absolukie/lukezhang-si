Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

Your contracts stay in your browser

FinePrint stores the contracts you paste, your saved analyses, checklist state, and flag feedback in your browser's local storage, on your own phone or computer. The analysis itself runs entirely on your device.

Device sync is on by default

To keep your saved analyses available on your other devices, copies are sent to our sync server at sync-proto.lukezhang.si. There is no identity system yet: anyone who holds your device key can read that data, so treat the key like a password. Analyses over 100 KB stay on the device that created them and are never uploaded.

To stop syncing, open the app, scroll to Device sync at the bottom, and check Pause sync. Paused means nothing leaves the device from then on. (Copies already on the server are not deleted by pausing; clear them by deleting the saved analysis in the app.)

Payments go through Stripe

Paid reviews ($15 one-time) are processed on Stripe's secure checkout page. FinePrint never sees or stores your card details. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to the payment step.

Review credits stay on the device you pay on

Your free first review and any purchased review credits are tracked in this browser only. They do not transfer to your other devices yet.

What we never do

No ads. No analytics trackers. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the sync server above, only while sync is unpaused.
