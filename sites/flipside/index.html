What is this

A rotational ambigram reads as one word right-side up and another — or the same — word rotated 180°. "swims" upside down is still "swims". "wow" upside down reads "mom".

Honest disclaimer

This is a design playground, not a generator. No algorithm truly invents ambigrams — they're hand-crafted, letter by letter. That's exactly what the tools here are for: flip to preview, overlay at low opacity to sketch, nudge and rotate letters until both readings click. The gallery words are natural ambigrams that already work; use them as starting points.

How to read one

Type any word and hit FLIP 180°. Watch what each letter becomes: d↔p, n↔u, m↔w, b↔q, while o, s, x, z, l, i stay themselves. Words built from those pairs are where ambigrams hide.
