How it works

  • Pair glyphs. Each letter is drawn from the classic rotational pair table: upright it reads as one letter, rotated 180° the same strokes read as its partner.
  • Merged strokes. A glyph is its letter’s skeleton plus its partner’s skeleton rotated 180°, drawn as one set of thick round strokes. That merged look is the authentic ambigram-generator aesthetic.
  • Honest failures. c, k, r, v have no rotational partner — Flipside tells you exactly which spots can’t work instead of faking it.
