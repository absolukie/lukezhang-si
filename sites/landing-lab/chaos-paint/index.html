chaos
drag anywhere on the canvas to fling it
The machinery: two rods, two equal masses, four coupled differential equations, integrated with RK4 at 240 steps per second. The trail color reads the tip speed in real time: teal when it drifts, hot pink when it panics. Chaotic systems never repeat, so every painting is a one of one.
