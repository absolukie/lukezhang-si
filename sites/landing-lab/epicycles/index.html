The machinery: the letters are sampled into 600 points, then a real discrete Fourier transform breaks the path into spinning circles. Each circle is one frequency, sized by its amplitude. Drop the count to 4 and watch my name melt into a blob, then crank it up and watch the math remember. Fewer circles, blurrier me.
