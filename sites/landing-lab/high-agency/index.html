The machinery

Three wheels

High agency runs on three wheels. Lose one and you wobble. Tap each for my take.

1

Clear thinking

+

Most problems are not mysteries. They are just unexamined. Before anything else, ask the filter question:

Does it defy physics? If no, it is a how problem, not a whether problem.

Someone has done the hard version of your thing before. Find them, copy the shape, adjust the details. Thinking clearly is mostly refusing to be confused on purpose.

2

Bias to action

+

Here is a secret that took me too long to learn:

There are no adults. Nobody is coming to approve it.

The meeting, the committee, the perfect plan. All of it is stalling with extra steps. Move first, apologize with results. A bad first version you can fix beats a perfect plan you never start.

3

Disagreeability

+

Polite people wait for permission. Polite people also wait in line, on hold, and for replies that never come.

Be willing to be slightly annoying. The call gets made by the person who calls twice.

This is not rudeness. It is refusing to let social friction decide your outcomes. Ask the dumb question. Send the follow-up. Knock twice.

There are no adults.

Does it defy physics?

Move first.
