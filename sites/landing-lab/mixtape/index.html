Side A
A1hello world (1998)
opening track. short, loud, mostly crying. sets the tone for everything after: show up, make noise, figure it out.
A2college radio static (2016)
four years of ramen, deadlines, and a sleep schedule that never recovered. the static is load-bearing.
A3staying in (2021)
the acoustic version. dinner, a card game, nowhere else to be. holds up on repeat.
A4the couch interlude (2022)
thirty seconds of room tone. no lyrics. somehow the most restful track on the tape.

"a great taco truck beats a mid restaurant every time."
-- liner note, probably

Side B
B1robinhood days
money stuff by day. steady beat, serious lyrics. pays for the studio time.
B2skill tree (single)
duolingo for leveling up your life. the design process is blogged here. radio edit.
B3rummy nights
liverpool rummy and get clocked, two card game apps. simple rules, hard to put down. the house favorite.
B4draw and guess (for two)
written for game night. two players, bad drawings, increasingly generous guesses.
B5ni hao (pinyin only)
beginner chinese, plain pinyin, no tone marks. tones are a known issue. the chorus is just dumpling orders.
B6unreleased demos
the kill list lives on another page. some tracks stay in the vault. hear what shipped.
CREDITS: written, built, and dubbed by luke zhang. builder. foodie. gamer. taste: simple but clever. recorded at home between day job and midnight. no samples were cleared because no audio exists.
