Privacy Policy — Liverpool Rummy

Effective October 4, 2026

What we collect

No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. We don't know your name, email, or anything that identifies you.

What stays on your device

The following live in your browser's local storage on your own device and never leave it:

  • Online-room session — the 4-letter room code, your player ID, and the display name you typed, so you can rejoin a room after a refresh.
  • Remove Ads entitlement — a flag that records you own the Remove Ads purchase on this device.
  • App preferences — cached ad feature-flag and developer overrides.

Online rooms

To play online, the app sends a 4-letter room code, your chosen display name, and your moves to our game server (a Cloudflare Worker) so it can act as the referee. This data lives only in the room's short-lived memory — it is not tied to an account, not sold, and not shared with anyone else. Server logs may record anonymous events like joins and errors for debugging.

Error reports

If the app crashes while you are in an online room, it may send a short crash report (error message and browser type) to the game server to help us fix bugs. This never happens in pass-and-play mode.

The ad flag check

On launch, the app checks an ad feature-flag service for one true/false value ("are ads on?"). The request carries no personal information. Ads are currently switched off; if enabled later, they will be our own cross-promo cards or the app store's own ads — no cross-app tracking.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, email, or payment details. The app only stores the yes/no result on your device.

Children

Liverpool Rummy is a general-audience card game and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Display names you choose are visible to other players in your room.
