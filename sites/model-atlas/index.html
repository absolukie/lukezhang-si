A curated atlas, not an exhaustive list. Prices are public list prices checked October 2026 and move fast; always check the provider before budgeting. Cost bands: Free means $0 (self-hosted open weights or a free tier). Cheap is under about $0.50 per million tokens or equivalent. Mid and Pricey go up from there. Embedding and image/video/audio models bill in different units, so bands compare typical usage cost, not identical units.
