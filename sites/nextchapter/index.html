Questions

Do family members need an account?
No. Your move manager sends a family digest: one file that opens on any phone and shows exactly what needs a decision. Answers come back by text or call, and the move team records each one with a timestamp.
We do estate cleanouts, not just senior moves.
Same product. Estate cleanouts add executor coordination, item-by-item disposition tracking, and probate-organized worksheet documentation, which Next Chapter handles natively.
What about donation receipts for taxes?
Every donated item is recorded with the receiving charity, date, and estimated value, ready to hand to the family or their accountant at tax time.
Is the family's data private?
Your move data is stored on this device, and each time you use the app it also sends a copy to our prototype sync service automatically. Details, including what is and is not encrypted, are on the Privacy page. Family links open on this device in this build. To reach family anywhere, send the family digest from the app.
