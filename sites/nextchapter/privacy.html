Privacy

Plain words about where your data lives. Last updated October 2026.

Your moves live on your device

Everything you enter in Next Chapter, client names, inventory, photos, vendors, donations, is stored in your browser's local storage, on the device you use. There is no account and no password. The one exception is the sync prototype, described below.

The sync prototype

Syncing starts on its own the first time you use the app. It sends a copy of your move records to our prototype sync service at sync-proto.lukezhang.si, so a second device with the same device key can pick up where you left off. There is no off switch in the prototype.

What the prototype is, and what it is not:

  • Your data travels over an encrypted connection, the same HTTPS your browser uses for everything else. It is not end-to-end encrypted: the service receives and stores the records it is sent, so anyone who can read the service's data can read them.
  • Devices are identified by a random key kept in this browser, not by user accounts. Anyone holding the key can read the device's records. There is no per-user identity and no way to revoke a key yet, so keep your device key to yourself the way you would a password.
  • Item photos never leave this device. Only text records are synced.
  • If the app hits an error, a short crash report is sent to the service so bugs can be fixed. Email addresses, phone numbers, and keys are scrubbed out before it is sent.

Family digest files

The family digest is a file you download and share yourself, by text, email, or print. Once you send it, it lives wherever you sent it, just like any document you share. Send it only to people the family trusts.

Backups and deletion

Back up: use Export backup on the home screen to download everything as a file you keep.

Delete everything: remove all moves from the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. That clears this device. The sync prototype keeps the copies it already received, and it has no self-serve wipe yet. To ask for those copies to be deleted, email us and a real person will help.
