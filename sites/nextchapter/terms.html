Terms

Plain language, no legalese. Last updated October 2026.

An organizer's worksheet

Next Chapter is a worksheet for organizing moves: inventory, family decisions, vendors, and donations. It is not legal advice and not tax advice. Donation values you enter are your estimates, not appraisals. Check anything that matters with the family's attorney or accountant.

Provided as-is, while in development

The app is under active development. We work hard to keep your data safe on your device, but we cannot promise it will never lose data or have bugs. Export backups regularly, especially before big milestones like move day.

Your clients' data is your responsibility

You enter real people's names, addresses, and photos. Keep their trust: share digests only with the family, follow the privacy rules of your state and profession, and comply with the law in everything you do with the app.

Fair use

Use Next Chapter for honest move management work. Do not try to break it, scrape it, or use it to harm anyone. We may update these terms as the product grows, and we will say so plainly when we do.
