How the score works

Score = expected balls to clear every orange, measured by simulating 64 full attempts with the game's real ball physics — a mix of random aim and a greedy "aim at the orange cluster" player. Plain balls only (no bucket refunds, powers, or Dream Burst), so it's conservative. Bands were calibrated from today's boards: the depth-1 heart scores ~8–10, typical normals ~11–13, elites ~15+.
