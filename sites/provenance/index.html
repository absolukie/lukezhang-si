The claim you can't prove is the claim you lose.

A $52,000 painting arrives with a hairline scratch. Was it there at receiving, or did it happen in your truck? Without timestamped evidence, the handler pays.

"The insurer asked for our condition report. We sent phone photos and a spreadsheet."

Every independent handler knows this moment. Paper forms get lost. Phone photos have no structure. Spreadsheets don't convince adjusters. Purpose-built condition software exists for museums with six-figure budgets, not for the two-truck white-glove shop.

Provenance is the version built for you: warehouse-floor fast, insurer-serious output.

Questions

Do I need special hardware?
No. It runs in the browser on the phone or tablet your crew already carries. Photos come from the camera or library.
Will insurers accept the dossier?
The dossier is built for that moment: timestamped photos, numbered findings with severity, chain of custody, and climate readings in one printable document. It gives the adjuster structured evidence instead of a photo dump.
We handle more than paintings.
The workflow is the same for watches, wine collections, instruments, classic cars, and other collectibles: register, photograph, pin the flaws, export the evidence.
What about our existing records?
Import and export are built in. Your records back up to a portable JSON file you own.
Is my clients' data private?
Records are stored on your device by default. Optional device sync copies record metadata (artwork details, pins, custody, and climate) to the prototype sync server so a second device can see them. Photo originals stay on the capturing device today.
