Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026. This is a plain-language summary of how the Provenance app handles your data today. It has not yet been reviewed by counsel; counsel review is pending before paid billing goes live.

The short version

Your records live in your browser, on your device. Full-resolution condition photos never leave the capturing device. Signing in and device sync are both optional. We sell nothing and run no ads or analytics beacons.

Data stored on your device

Provenance keeps its working database in your browser's local storage on the device you use: artwork records, compressed condition photos, condition pins, custody events, climate readings, loan terms, your signature image, and the examiner name you type. Nothing is uploaded by default. If you delete the browser data, the records are gone, so export a backup before clearing anything.

Photos stay on the capturing device

Condition photos are compressed and stored on the device that captured them. Even when device sync is on, full photo bytes are stripped before anything is sent and stay local only. A photo captured on another device shows as image bytes unavailable, and the app says so everywhere instead of silently substituting something else.

Optional device sync (prototype)

If you link devices with a device key, record metadata (titles, notes, pins, custody, climate, photo metadata such as captions and integrity hashes, but not full photo bytes) is sent to our sync prototype server to keep your devices in agreement. Treat the device key like a password: anyone who has it can read those records.

Optional sign-in

Sign-in is optional; the app works fully without it. If you sign in with an email magic link, Apple, Google, or phone code, we store the email address or phone number you provide so we can authenticate you. We never claim to verify anyone's identity; the examiner name on a dossier is self-asserted.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us, and we never see or store card numbers. Billing is currently in test mode; no real money moves.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no sale or rental of your data.
  • No analytics beacons or tracking pixels in the app today.
  • No location tracking; photo coordinates are captured only when you allow it, and the photo saves normally without them.
