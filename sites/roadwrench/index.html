Questions

Does RoadWrench replace my invoicing?

No. It's the warranty and insurance documentation layer, not the register. It makes the claim packet; your invoices stay where they are.

Does it work without signal at a campground?

Yes. Everything is stored in your browser and works offline. Your data is there when the bars come back.

Which warranty companies does it work with?

Any of them. The packet is a standard claim document: insurer, claim and auth numbers, complaint/cause/correction, parts, labor, photos, signatures. You fill in the insurer's details per job.

Who owns my data?

You do. It's stored locally in your browser. Export a backup anytime from Setup. Nothing is sold, ever.

Can my customers sign on my phone?

Yes. The packet has on-screen signature pads for customer and tech, saved right into the document.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly billing, no contract, no cancellation maze.
