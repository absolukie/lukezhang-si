Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

Your data lives on your device

RoadWrench stores your jobs, photos, signatures, and company profile in your browser's local storage, on your own phone or computer. Full-size photos never leave the device that took them.

Optional device sync

Sync is off unless you turn it on. If you enable it, job data (including photo thumbnails and signatures) is sent to our prototype sync server at sync-proto.lukezhang.si so your other devices can see it. There is no identity system yet: whoever holds your device key can read that data, so treat the key like a password.

What we never do

No ads. No analytics trackers. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the sync server above, only when you turn sync on.

Backups are your responsibility

Export a backup from Setup regularly. If you lose your device or clear your browser data without a backup, your jobs are gone and we cannot recover them.
