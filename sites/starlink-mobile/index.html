SpaceX announcement · Oct 8, 2026

Starlink wants to be your phone company

SpaceX agreed to buy the rights to a slice of radio airwaves covering the whole US. That one purchase is the missing piece that lets satellites talk directly to your normal phone. No dish. No new phone. Here is how it works.

Agreement announced · FCC approval still pending

The missing piece

Think of it like radio stations. Two stations cannot broadcast on the same frequency, and a satellite network cannot talk to your phone without its own licensed slice of the air. SpaceX had almost everything else:

The fine print

  • Not done yet. The 800 MHz transfer still needs final FCC approval before Starlink Mobile can use it.
  • Not tomorrow. Analysts do not expect Starlink Mobile to dent AT&T, Verizon, or T-Mobile until around 2029 at the earliest. Building a phone company takes years, even from orbit.
  • Not a launch. This was a business announcement, not a rocket. No new satellites went up for this.
  • Not free money either. Terms were not disclosed; the Wall Street Journal valued the deal at around $8 billion.
