Is Airbnb legal in Breckenridge, Colorado?

Legal with a license, but residential zones are over cap with a 205-person waitlist. Breckenridge requires an Accommodation Unit License for stays under 30 days, costing $250 base plus $756 per bedroom per year in regulatory fees plus a $75 to $175 BOLT tax. The town is split into four STR zones: the Resort zone has no cap, but the Residential Area zone is over its 390-license cap with a 205-applicant waitlist and no new licenses until attrition. Licenses die at sale, so buying a licensed home gives you no license.

What could change this verdict

Your HOA
HOA and deed restrictions can ban STRs even in an uncapped zone.
Buying a licensed home
The license is surrendered at closing; in a capped zone the buyer joins the back of the waitlist.
Legal with permit

Primary-residence rentals of 21 days or fewer skip the per-bedroom fee

If the unit is your primary residence and rents 21 or fewer days a year, you can get an exemption from the $756 per-bedroom regulatory fee, though the base license and BOLT still apply.
