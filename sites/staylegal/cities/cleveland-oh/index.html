Key limits

Density cap
STRs are limited to at least one, or no more than 10% of the residential units on a block or in a multi-unit building; exceeding it needs a Board of Zoning Appeals variance.
Insurance
$500,000 in liability insurance is required.
Local contact
A designated local contact must be able to arrive at the property within one hour of a complaint.
Nuisance rules
Three nuisance incidents in 12 months revoke the license, and unlicensed operation carries up to a $5,000 civil penalty.

Taxes

County bed tax
6.5% Cuyahoga County bed tax, collected by Airbnb automatically
City lodging tax
City transient occupancy taxes apply per the new ordinance; confirm current remittance rules with the city

Registration checklist

  1. 1
    Check the block cap
    Confirm the block or building is under the 10% STR density cap before applying.
  2. 2
    Apply with insurance
    File for the license with proof of $500,000 in liability insurance and a designated local contact.
  3. 3
    Keep it compliant
    Track nuisance complaints carefully, since three in a year cost you the license.

What could change this verdict

Your HOA
Condo and HOA rules can ban STRs independently of the city license, so check the governing documents.
Rule changes
Ohio has active state preemption bills that could strip municipal STR authority, so the local rules may change.
