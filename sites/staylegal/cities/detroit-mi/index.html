Is Airbnb legal in Detroit, Michigan?

No STR ordinance on the books, but rental registration plus inspection is mandatory. Detroit has never adopted a short-term rental ordinance, so there is no city STR license, night cap, or guest limit. A whole house you do not live in counts as rental property under Chapter 8 of the city code, which means registering with BSEED, passing a 15-point safety inspection, and holding a Certificate of Compliance before you collect rent.

Permit and costs

Permit
Rental Registration + Certificate of Compliance
Issued by
City of Detroit Buildings, Safety Engineering and Environmental Department (BSEED)
Cost
Registration free; inspection $195-$225 + $20 processing
Renewal
Keep the certificate current; re-inspect as required by the city

Key limits

No night cap
No annual night limit or STR-specific guest limit currently exists, since there is no STR ordinance.
Inspection required
A 15-point safety inspection is required before renting, and properties without a certificate of compliance can be ticketed.
Compliance push
The city is pushing to bring rentals up to code by September 2027, so enforcement is tightening even without an STR law.
Draft rules pending
Draft short-term rental language sits with the City Council's Legislative Policy Division and could become law.

Taxes

State use tax
Michigan use tax on lodging, which Airbnb remits on hosts' behalf
City income tax
Detroit income tax applies to rental income and is filed separately through the city's Income Tax Division

Registration checklist

  1. 1
    Register with BSEED
    Register the property as a rental with the Buildings, Safety Engineering and Environmental Department.
  2. 2
    Pass the inspection
    Schedule and pass the 15-point safety inspection, with the first re-inspection included in the fee.
  3. 3
    Get the certificate
    Hold the Certificate of Compliance in hand before taking any bookings.

What could change this verdict

Your HOA
Deed restrictions and condo bylaws can ban STRs even where the city has no rule, so check them before buying.
Rule changes
The council's draft STR ordinance could add licensing, occupancy limits, and night caps, so watch City Council action.
Legal

Renting rooms inside your own home is excluded from registration

Owner-occupied room rentals are exempt from the Chapter 8 rental registration and inspection requirement.
