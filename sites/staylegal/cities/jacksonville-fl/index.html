Is Airbnb legal in Jacksonville, Florida?

Legal, no city STR permit required. Jacksonville has no enforceable short-term rental certificate. Courts ruled in 2022 and 2024 that the city's old zoning treatment conflicted with state preemption. An investor needs a Florida DBPR vacation rental license and a Duval County Local Business Tax Receipt, and that is the whole paperwork stack.

Registration checklist

  1. 1
    Get the state DBPR license
    Apply for a vacation rental dwelling or condo license from the DBPR Division of Hotels and Restaurants.
  2. 2
    Get the Duval County business tax receipt
    Apply with the Duval County Tax Collector, the receipt runs October 1 to September 30.
  3. 3
    Register for the tourist tax
    Register to collect and remit the 6% Duval County tourist development tax.

Hosted stays need no extra permit either

Jacksonville does not distinguish hosted from non-hosted rentals. Room shares need only the same state license and county business tax receipt.
