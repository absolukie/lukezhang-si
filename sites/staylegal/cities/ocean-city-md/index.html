Is Airbnb legal in Ocean City, Maryland?

STR-friendly with an annual town license. Ocean City allows short-term rentals with an annual rental license plus a noise permit and a supplemental STR license, costing about $141 to $191 a year plus per-room fees. The town ended its moratorium on new licenses in residential zones in 2026, and there is no owner-occupancy requirement. Your license control number must appear on every listing.
