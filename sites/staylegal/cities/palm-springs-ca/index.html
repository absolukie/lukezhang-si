Is Airbnb legal in Palm Springs, California?

Investor-friendly permit — with contract caps. Palm Springs welcomes investor STRs with no owner-occupancy requirement, but caps you at 26 rental contracts per year and limits new certificates to 20% of homes per neighborhood.
