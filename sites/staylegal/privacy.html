Privacy

Plain words about where your data lives. Last updated October 2026.

Everything stays on this device

StayLegal has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you enter or tap in the app is stored in your browser's local storage, on the device you use:

  • Addresses you check and the verdicts you save
  • Registration checklist ticks, night-tracker counts, and permit renewal dates
  • HOA triage answers
  • City requests, including the draft that autosaves while you type and the optional email address. Your requests are never sent anywhere; the download button exports them as a JSON file you keep

Nothing you type is sent to a server. The page itself makes no third-party network requests. The source links under each verdict are ordinary outbound links: tapping one is the only time your browser contacts another website, and only the ones you choose.

No tracking

The app keeps a small usage log on the device (the last 200 actions, like checks run and quiz completions) so usage can be measured without accounts. That log never leaves the device. There are no beacons, no third-party analytics, and no advertising identifiers.

Share links are private by default

A copied share link points at the city-level verdict, with no address in it. Your address is included only if you explicitly tick "Include my address in the link" before copying.

No sync, no server copy

The public build has no sync feature. There is no server copy of your checks, and your data does not follow you to another device. If you clear this site's data, it is gone.

Backups and deletion

Back up: use "Download my requests" on the request form to export your city requests as JSON. Saved checks can be re-run any time from a fresh address check.

Delete everything: delete your saved checks inside the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Either way removes it from this device only.

Research summaries, not legal advice

StayLegal shows research summaries drawn from city ordinances and official guides. It is a starting point, not legal advice, and it does not track what you do with it.
