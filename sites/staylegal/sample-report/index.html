Legal with a city permit, primary residence only

Permit: Home-Sharing Registration, $89 / year

LA allows home-sharing only in your primary residence (where you live 6 plus months a year), capped at 120 nights per year. A dedicated investment property you do not live in is not eligible.

Registration checklist

  1. 1
    Prove primary residency
    Government ID plus utility bills, deed, or lease showing you live there. Start at LA Dept. of City Planning
  2. 2
    Register online
    File through the Home-Sharing portal at planning.lacity.gov and pay $89.
  3. 3
    Post your registration number
    It must appear on every listing, even while approval is pending.
  4. 4
    Keep records 3 years
    The city can audit your hosted nights against the 120-night cap.

What could change this verdict

A different city nearby
Santa Monica, West Hollywood, and Beverly Hills each have their own, often stricter, rules. The address is the whole verdict.
Extended Home-Sharing
After 6 months registered (or 60 hosted nights) you can apply to exceed 120 nights: $850/yr administrative clearance.
Your HOA or lease
An HOA or lease can ban short-term rentals even where the city allows them.

What we assumed

Investor perspective. This report answers for a non-owner-occupant buying a whole home to Airbnb. The hosted path (you live there) follows the same permit but counts differently against the cap.
City-level rules only. The verdict comes from the city ordinance and official guides, not a parcel lookup. Zoning overlays, specific-plan areas, and interim ordinances can change the answer for one lot.
Single-family home. Condos, rent-stabilized units, and newer ADUs face extra restrictions or are ineligible outright.

What we did not check

Parcel zoning overlay. We did not verify this lot's zoning or any specific-plan restrictions.
HOA and CC&Rs status. We did not review the building's documents. Run the HOA red-flag check and read the CC&Rs before you buy.
Building-specific bans. Some buildings opt out or are flagged by the city independently of the ordinance.
Exact renewal timing. Renewal windows shift; confirm the current deadline with the planning department.
