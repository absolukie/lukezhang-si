DEMO FINDINGS — verify before acting.

1. Foreigners CAN register on 12306 (China Railway) — passport verification required, done in-app or at a station counter.
2. Registration needs: passport scan + a Chinese mobile number for SMS. eSIMs with Chinese numbers generally work; some travelers use a travel companion's number.
3. Verify identity early — approval can take 1–3 days. Do it before Dec, not the week of travel.
4. Alternative: Trip.com / China Highlights book the same trains with foreign passports and no 12306 account, for a small fee.
