UI Candy

Tasty little components for AI-native interfaces. Every one runs live below — poke it, then steal the code. Vanilla JS, no dependencies, no build step.

01

Snap Carousel

The Kit

One card at a time, mandatory snap, arrows in the gutters, N/total counter. Tapping a card acts in place — the page never scrolls, cards never change height.

02

Step-Through

The Kit

Walk a process one tap at a time, Glide feel. The Next button is pinned — its resting position is pixel-identical on every step, even mid rapid-fire.

03

Before / After

The Kit

Drag the Notch divider 1:1 with your finger. Tap anywhere to glide there. The container never changes size, so dragging can't shift the page.

04

Morph Button

The Kit

One button, two sequential actions. Tap Copy — on success it springs into Share. Type anything and it morphs right back. WAAPI choreography, interruptible mid-flight.

05

A/B Toggle

The Kit

One switch, every tap flips — no side-picking. The thumb always shows what's on screen, and the stage crossfades beneath it.

06

Decision Board

The Kit

The "goated" flow: tappable options, per-question notes, everything autosaves. Copy your picks when you're done deciding.

07

Data Viz

For comparisons where the interesting difference is tiny. Each bar gets a magnified inset strip so the sliver you actually care about becomes visible.

08

Data Viz

N dots = a population. Flip scenarios and watch the outcome ripple through, staggered so your eye can follow every dot.

09

Data Viz
